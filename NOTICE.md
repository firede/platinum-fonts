## Platinum Mono (mono)

Latin and symbols: [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font) 2026.09.25, 12px monospaced, with the
East Asian glyphs it draws otherwise than cjk12. Each glyph is set in the
cells of its character, a column wider and two rows taller than the
source's; glyphs too wide for their cells are left out.

Drawn for this repository: box drawing, blocks, braille and Powerline
separators, by [internal/grid](internal/grid), and other glyphs, in
[glyphs/mono.txt](glyphs/mono.txt). Copyright (c) 2026, Firede (https://github.com/firede).

Ligatures: [AKR Pixel](https://github.com/AkaraChen/font) 61efafc, widened to Mono's cells and cut into a
slice per cell. MIT License, [licenses/akr-pixel/LICENSE.txt](licenses/akr-pixel/LICENSE.txt): Copyright (c) 2026 AkaraChen.

Icons: [Nerd Fonts](https://github.com/ryanoasis/nerd-fonts) v3.5.1, rasterized from Symbols Nerd Font onto the
cells; the most used are redrawn after the originals for this repository,
in [glyphs/icons.txt](glyphs/icons.txt). Nerd Fonts is under the MIT License, [licenses/nerd-fonts/LICENSE.txt](licenses/nerd-fonts/LICENSE.txt). The icon sets
it gathers are under the licenses it lists for them:
