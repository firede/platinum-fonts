# Design

English | [简体中文](design.zh.md)

Every family is drawn on a 12-pixel em. The Latin of Platinum Sans and
Platinum Sans Small is drawn for this repository, in [`glyphs`](../glyphs).
The East Asian glyphs, and the Latin of Platinum Mono, come from fonts
pinned in [`sources.toml`](../sources.toml). [NOTICE.md](../NOTICE.md)
credits each source.

## Metrics

In pixels. Ascent and descent are the rows above and below the baseline
that a line takes, as `Face.Ascent` and `Face.Descent` report them.

| Family | Ascent | Descent | Cap height | x-height |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Platinum Sans | 9 | 3 | 9 | 6 |
| Platinum Sans Small | 8 | 3 | 8 | 5 |
| Platinum Mono | 11 | 3 | 8 | 6 |
| East Asian (`cjk12`) | 13 | 3 | | |

## Platinum Sans and Platinum Sans Small

Both cover Latin-1, the common Central European letters and typographic
punctuation. Platinum Sans also has the symbols ⌘ ↑ ↵ ⌫.

Platinum Sans Bold is drawn by hand. Platinum Sans Small Bold is its
Regular emboldened: each glyph drawn again one pixel to the right, so one
pixel wider.

Accented letters are composed from a letter and a mark; the format of the
drawings, marks included, is described in
[`internal/drawn`](../internal/drawn/drawn.go).

## East Asian glyphs

The East Asian glyphs are those of [Fusion Pixel Font](https://github.com/TakWolf/fusion-pixel-font),
12px, proportional, unmodified. There is a set for each region: SC, TC, JP
and KR. They hold the same characters, about 34,600, drawn in that
region's forms. [Coverage](coverage.md) lists how much of each national
character set they cover.

An ideograph is 11×11 pixels of ink, from one row below the baseline to
ten above it, and advances 12 pixels.

Bold is Regular emboldened, with the gaps of one pixel between strokes
kept open so that dense characters stay legible.

## Platinum Mono

### Cells

A cell is 7 pixels wide and 14 tall: 11 rows above the baseline and 3
below, the space between lines included. A character takes the cells
that `wcwidth` gives it: two for East Asian Wide and Fullwidth, none for
marks, and one for the rest, East Asian Ambiguous included. A glyph too
wide for its cells is left out, so that the terminal's fallback font
draws it instead.

The first column of each cell is kept clear, as the space between
characters. Only lines that run on into the next cell ink it: a dash, a
box drawing line, a ligature.

### Latin and symbols

Latin, Greek, Cyrillic and most symbols are those of Fusion Pixel Font
12px monospaced. They are drawn on cells of 6×12, and are set a column to
the right in Mono's cells of 7×14, on the same baseline.

About a hundred frequent symbols that it draws two cells wide or lacks,
such as — → ■ ✓ ≠, are drawn in one cell in
[`glyphs/mono.txt`](../glyphs/mono.txt).

Box drawing, blocks, shades, braille and the Powerline separators are
generated by [`internal/grid`](../internal/grid), so that they fill their
cells and meet their neighbours. Shades lie on slanted lattices that
repeat every 7 columns, so that adjacent cells join without a seam. A
braille dot is 2×2 pixels.

### East Asian glyphs

Chinese, Japanese and Korean are the `cjk12` glyphs of the region, set a
column to the right. As in the other families, they reach one row below
the baseline, so the Latin and East Asian text of a line keep a common
baseline and a row clear above and below.

### Bold

Bold is Regular emboldened within the cells: each glyph drawn again one
pixel to the right, keeping the gaps between strokes and the first column
clear. The generated glyphs and the icons are the same in both styles.

### Ligatures

The 31 ligatures, such as `=>` `!=` `<=` `->` `::` `/*`, come from
[AKR Pixel](https://github.com/AkaraChen/font). They are drawn on cells of
6×12 and widened like the Latin. Each is cut into a slice per cell, so
every glyph still advances one cell and the text stays aligned.

A ligature is not joined where the character before it repeats its first
or the one after repeats its last, so runs such as `====` and `....` stay
apart. In the font files the ligatures are the OpenType
feature `calt`.

### Icons

The icons are the 10,600 or so of [Nerd Fonts](https://www.nerdfonts.com)
3.5.1, in the Private Use Area. They are rasterized from Symbols Nerd Font
with its line height mapped to the cell's 14 rows, and centred on two
cells; the Powerline symbols keep their place in the cell.

Rasterized at this size, detailed icons lose their shape. The 170 or so
used most, by eza, Starship and the Powerline prompts, are drawn anew in
[`glyphs/icons.txt`](../glyphs/icons.txt): folders and files, languages,
operating systems, Git and status. They are drawn in 12×11 pixels,
centred on the middle of the ideographs.

Each icon advances one cell and draws up to two, as in the Nerd Font
fonts (not Nerd Font Mono).
